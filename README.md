# RelayContext

**Never lose your AI conversation when you hit a limit.**

RelayContext is a free Chrome extension that lets you switch between ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, and Grok without losing context. Capture your conversation in one click — RelayContext automatically opens your next AI in a new tab and continues right where you left off.

No accounts. No servers. No data leaving your browser.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Auto platform detection** — Opens on ChatGPT? Claude? Gemini? RelayContext detects the platform automatically and scrapes it the right way.
- **Auto-opens the next platform** — Pick where you want to continue and RelayContext opens it in a new tab and drops the context in for you. No manual copy-paste required.
- **Works across all major AIs** — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, and Grok — switch in any direction, any time.
- **100% local & private** — No servers, no accounts, no data collection. Everything runs in your browser. Your conversations stay yours.
- **Instant** — From hitting the limit to being ready to continue in a new AI takes less than 3 seconds.
- **Smart prompt format** — The generated context includes full conversation history with clear user/assistant labels so the new AI has complete context.

## 🧠 How it works

1. **Hit the limit** — You're deep in a conversation on any supported AI and need to switch.
2. **Click & capture** — Open RelayContext. One click captures the full conversation.
3. **Auto-open & continue** — Pick your next AI. RelayContext opens it in a new tab and continues automatically — no manual pasting.

## 🚀 Installation

1. [Download the latest release (.zip)](https://github.com/aadarshyadav7439/Relay-Context/archive/refs/tags/v1.0.0.zip)
2. Unzip the downloaded file.
3. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions`.
4. Enable **Developer mode** (top right toggle).
5. Click **Load unpacked** and select the unzipped folder.
6. Pin the extension to your toolbar — you're done ✓

Works in Chrome and any Chromium-based browser (Edge, Brave, Opera, etc.).

## 🖱️ Usage

1. Have a conversation on any supported AI platform.
2. When you're ready to switch (or hit a usage limit), click the RelayContext icon in your toolbar.
3. Confirm the capture — RelayContext will detect the platform and pull in the conversation.
4. Choose which AI to continue on. RelayContext opens it in a new tab and carries the context over automatically.

## 🔒 Privacy

RelayContext runs entirely in your browser. It does not use any external servers, does not require an account, and does not collect, store, or transmit your conversation data anywhere. Captured context stays local to your device for the duration of the handoff.
